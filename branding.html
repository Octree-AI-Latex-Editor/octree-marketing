Octree — Brand System v1

Structure, made light

Octree turns conversations into typeset documents. The identity is built on one idea — recursive structure — expressed as an octagonal O, lit from above. The blue is Editorial Blue #0F62FE — chosen for its working-tool seriousness and 5:1 contrast with white text.

Logo

The mark is the G2 icon: an octagon ring on a vertical gradient tile, light falling from directly above. The wordmark is always Space Grotesk Medium, lowercase, tracked −0.02em. Never letterspace it, never bold it, never set it in another face.

Motion

One entrance animation: Dot bloom. A dot is born at the center, grows, then hollows open as it expands into the ring — one continuous motion — while a soft white glow breathes once mid-expansion. ~1.5s total. Use for splash, sign-in, and project-open. Always honor prefers-reduced-motion by showing the static logo.

Dot bloom · click to replay
Spec
One stroked midline octagon, gradient from frame one: born as a dot (scale 0.04, stroke 41.6 = solid), grows solid to 40%, then stroke thins to 14.4 as scale reaches 1 — the aperture opens during expansion · 0.9s · cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1) · 0.05s delay
Gradient ring swap: opacity fade 0.2s at 0.9s (pixel-identical, invisible)
Glow: white stroke 6, blur 5px, peaks at 55% · 0.9s ease-in-out · starts at 0.4s (mid-expansion)
Corners: miter throughout — never rounded

Voice

Plain verbs, sentence case, no filler. Buttons say what happens: "Compile PDF", not "Submit". The product never says "please wait" — it says what it's doing.
